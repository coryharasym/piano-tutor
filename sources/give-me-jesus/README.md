# Give Me Jesus: traditional spiritual, Songbook arrangement

This is the traditional African American spiritual beginning “In the morning
when I rise”, not the Crosby/Sweney hymn or a modern commercial setting.
Hymnary identifies the underlying tune as public domain, and documents the
traditional text separately:

- https://hymnary.org/tune/give_me_jesus_spiritual
- https://hymnary.org/text/in_the_morning_when_i_rise_in_the_morn

The melody was checked visually against the vocal line of Grace Immanuel's
hymnal score (the first two systems and the upper line of its refrain):
https://s3.amazonaws.com/graceimmanuel.gracemusic/Hymnal_2nd-ed/pdfs/Give%20Me%20Jesus.pdf

Only the traditional melody is transcribed in melody.json. No harmony, engraving,
recording, or modern artist arrangement from that edition is included. The source
key is B-flat; the teaching version transposes it up a whole tone to C. Its three
dotted-eighth/sixteenth pickup figures become even eighths. The other note values,
pickup, melodic contours, melismas and ties are retained. One verse and refrain
are performed once; the last tied note is followed by the reference quarter rest.

The left hand is a newly written single-note bass, with no blocked chords, pedal
requirement or simultaneous octave grip. Dynamics, phrasing, fingering and all
practice guidance are newly authored. Finger changes on repeated notes prepare
small position shifts; the right hand should move rather than stretch from C to A.

The piece is an optional Beginner Hymns/Songbook item associated with Chapter 5,
Playing with expression. It does not add a course lesson, change existing lesson
IDs, replace a recital or modify course completion totals. Chapter PDFs continue
to document the unchanged curriculum. The app can print the complete score.
